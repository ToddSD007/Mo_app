# How to use your own dice

This mode allows you to perform the cast yourself using physical dice. You do not need special Mo dice. Regular six-sided dice can be used and entered according to the key.

Before rolling, pause for a moment and let the mind settle.

Bring one clear question gently to mind. In the traditional practice, the quality of the question matters deeply. The clearer and more sincere the intention, the more meaningful the answer tends to be.

You may wish to briefly visualize Manjushri before you cast, or softly recite the mantra:

**OM AH RA PA TSA NA DHI**

The mantra is traditionally understood as a way of gathering and protecting the mind, orienting it toward wisdom and clarity.

When ready, roll two dice.

The order matters:

- the first die becomes the first syllable
- the second die becomes the second syllable

Enter the result exactly as rolled.

## Number to syllable key

1 — DHI
2 — RA
3 — PA
4 — NA
5 — TSA
6 — AH

## Optional firmness cast

You may also perform a second cast to determine how firmly the answer should be held.

If entered, the app will include this in the final reading.
